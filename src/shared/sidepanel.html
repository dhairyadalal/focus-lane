How to practice box breathing

  1. Step 1: Exhale. Sit up straight and slowly let all the air out of your lungs through your mouth.
  2. Step 2: Inhale. Breathe in slowly and deeply through your nose to a count of four, filling your lungs completely.
  3. Step 3: Hold. Hold the air in your lungs for a slow count of four.
  4. Step 4: Pause. Exhale slowly through your mouth for four seconds, then hold your empty lungs for another count of four.
